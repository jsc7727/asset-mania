# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback,
and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized
language or imagery, publishing private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository administrator through the private contact route
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially. Maintainers
may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may apply warnings, temporary bans, or permanent
bans as appropriate.

This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
