# Third-Party Notices

## Initial status

No third-party source, assets, model weights, datasets, or binary fixtures are currently
included. Development dependencies are resolved in `uv.lock`; their license notices will be
reviewed before a source release.

## Inventory policy

Before adding a third-party package, asset, icon, texture, HDRI, fixture, or model reference,
record its source, version or immutable revision, license, required notice, and redistribution
evidence here. Model terms are recorded separately from software licenses. Every tracked binary
fixture must also have an entry in `tests/fixtures/PROVENANCE.md` when that fixture system lands.
